# Community conduct

Treat contributors, students and reviewers with respect. Give evidence-based feedback about the work rather than the person. Welcome questions and acknowledge uncertainty. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, share private information or misrepresent scientific results.

Keep patient information and credentials out of discussions, issues, screenshots and demonstrations. Respect source licenses, attribution and research integrity.

The repository maintainer may moderate comments, restrict participation or remove inappropriate material. For sensitive concerns, request a private contact route without posting the sensitive details publicly. Security concerns follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
