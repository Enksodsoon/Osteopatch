Make the review
process visible.

OsteoPatch separates what the source says, what the model suggests and what the reviewer decides. A correction becomes a new review event. It never silently rewrites the prediction.

  1. Find a patch

    Browse source images and prioritize uncertain model outputs for educational review.

  2. Inspect the evidence

    Zoom into H&E, compare three uncalibrated scores and inspect qualified attribution.

  3. Make a decision

    Accept, correct or defer. Mixed tissue and poor quality remain review states.

  4. Keep the history

    Preserve revisions and export the model suggestion alongside human review.

Useful software.
Honest limitations.

The strongest part of this project is an inspectable review workflow—not a claim that a small experimental classifier is clinically reliable.

The frozen evaluation has a particularly weak viable-tumor class. The original model binary is absent; the recovered attribution head has a separate identity.
