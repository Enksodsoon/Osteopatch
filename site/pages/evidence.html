What the evaluation does not establish

Independence was assessed over only four case/slide groups; patient-level independence is unverified. One fold is single-class, and some group/class combinations have extremely small support. Patches are not independent biological samples. The model’s scores are uncalibrated, not disease probabilities.

The full local source collection contains 1,144 patches. The evaluation uses $evaluation_rows eligible patches. The workshop demo presents 50 selected images. These counts describe different populations and must not be interchanged.

Attribution is not segmentation

Contrastive Grad-CAM describes model behavior under a particular implementation. It does not establish tissue boundaries, biological causality or diagnostic correctness. Missing or invalid attribution must be disclosed rather than replaced with a fabricated heatmap.
